1 — Welcome

Thanks for reading the DiceLab benchmark report. This is a cross-language experiment where I implemented the same dice-rolling simulation in five languages — C++, Go, Java, Python, and Rust — and then measured how they compare under identical workloads.

The report is organised around three questions:

  • How do the five implementations compare at the same workload size?
  • How does each implementation scale as rolls grow from 100 to 1 million?
  • How consistent (repeatable) is each language's timing across many trials?

Everything here is interactive. Charts are powered by Plotly.js, so you can hover for details, zoom into regions, and toggle traces on and off via the legend. Several sections include dropdowns to switch between workloads or languages. I’ve also included my own commentary throughout — look for the blue “Author’s take” boxes.
