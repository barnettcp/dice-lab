Workload Distribution Ridgeline

Each ridge below shows the full timing distribution (KDE) at a given workload level, with the largest workload (1 M rolls) at the bottom. A dot on each baseline marks the mean. Hover anywhere on a ridge for summary statistics (mean, std, CV). Distributions that narrow and shift left as workloads decrease indicate stable, predictable scaling.
